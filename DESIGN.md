# Design System: Vercel

## Philosophy
- Clean, utilitarian, and distraction-free. Swiss typography meets technical precision.
- Elimination of AI slop: zero unnecessary gradients, zero decorative floating shapes, zero pill cards without clear functional boundaries.

## Color Tokens
- Background: #000000 (dark) or #FFFFFF (light)
- Foreground: #EDEDED (dark) or #111111 (light)
- Muted / Neutral: #888888
- Accent: #0070F3 (system blue), #50E3C2 (cyan)
- Borders: 1px solid #333333 (dark) or #EAEAEA (light)
- Card Surfaces: #0A0A0A (dark) or #FAFAFA (light)

## Typography & Structure
- Font Family: Geist, Geist Mono, Inter, -apple-system, sans-serif
- Grid: Crisp 1px border grid divisions separating sections and data cells
- Metrics & Tables: Monospaced values with high data density and subtle labels
- Transitions: Snappy, minimal (150ms-200ms ease), no bouncy physics
